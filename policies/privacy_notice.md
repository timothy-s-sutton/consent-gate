# Larkspur Financial Privacy Notice

> FICTIONAL DOCUMENT. Larkspur Financial is not a real company. This notice exists only to demonstrate the consent-gate project and is not legal advice.

This notice explains what personal information Larkspur Financial collects, why we use it, the legal basis for each use, and the choices and rights our customers have.

## Information we collect

We collect identity and contact details such as name, email address, phone number, and mailing state. We collect account data such as Social Security number (SSN), payment card numbers, account segment, and the history of service interactions, including notes written by our staff during calls. We record each customer's consent and preference choices, including where and when the choice was made (web form, call center, or mobile app).

## Purposes of processing and legal basis

Every use of customer data must have a defined purpose and a legal basis. Not every purpose requires consent, but every purpose requires a basis. Staff and automated systems, including AI agents, may only use data for the purpose they declare.

### Servicing

We use customer data to answer a customer's own questions, resolve problems, and operate their accounts. The legal basis is the performance of our contract with the customer. Servicing does not require marketing consent.

### Fraud prevention

We use customer data to detect, investigate, and prevent fraud and financial crime. The legal basis is our legal obligations and our legitimate interest in protecting customers and the bank. Fraud prevention does not require consent.

### Marketing

We use contact details, including email addresses, to send offers and campaigns only when the customer has granted marketing consent. The legal basis is consent. If marketing consent is denied or was never collected, the customer must not be included in any marketing audience or campaign, by email or any other channel. A customer who has opted out of the sale or sharing of personal information is also excluded from marketing audiences, even if marketing consent was granted.

### Analytics

We analyze customer behavior in aggregate to improve products. The legal basis is consent. Customers whose analytics consent is denied or not collected are excluded from analytics data sets.

## Consent and preference management

Consent is recorded per customer and per purpose, with a status of granted, denied, or not collected. Not collected is treated the same as denied: no consent means no use for consent-based purposes. Consent choices are honored as soon as they are recorded, and downstream systems must read the current record at the time of use rather than relying on an old copy.

## Do not sell or share my personal information

Customers in states with consumer privacy laws, such as California under the CCPA as amended by the CPRA, may opt out of the sale or sharing of their personal information. Larkspur honors this opt-out for every customer regardless of state. When a customer has opted out of sale or sharing, we do not include them in marketing audiences or share their data with advertising partners, and this opt-out overrides any marketing consent.

## Your privacy rights

Customers may ask to know what personal information we hold, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it where the law allows, and opt out of marketing at any time. Customers will not be treated differently for exercising these rights. Requests can be made through the mobile app, the web form, or the call center, and we verify identity before acting on a request.

## Sharing with service providers

We share personal information with service providers only under contract, only for the purposes listed in this notice, and only the minimum data needed. Service providers may not use the data for their own purposes.

## Contact

Questions about this notice can be sent to the Larkspur Privacy Office through the web form or the call center.
